# Connoisseur // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Connoisseur, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Connoisseur/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Connoisseur/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on Classic Era, then `/reload` and run it again on TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **This build installed on both flavors while the game is fully closed.** This release renames some of the add-on's files, and a `/reload` does not pick that up; only restarting the game does.
- **A character that was already using the previous release (2026.08.30.A)**, with at least two named Restock Lists and the Restocker window dragged somewhere new and resized, plus **a second character on the same account** following one of those lists. Do **not** delete your SavedVariables before this run. Keeping existing lists intact is the riskiest thing this release does, and you only get one chance to watch it.
- **A Warlock** with talent points spent and a Soulstone in its bags.
- **A Rogue with Poisons trained**, carrying poison, with a poison on its Restock List set above what it carries. On Classic Era it must be **level 34 or higher**.
- **A Paladin or Shaman of level 30 or higher.** Only a Shaman shows the flavor difference.
- **A Mage** carrying a Mana Gem, and **a Demonic Rune or Dark Rune** your characters are high enough level to use.
- **A second player.** Step 8 needs them on a Warlock, step 11 needs them to join and leave your group, and on TBC Anniversary step 9 needs you both at level 70 for an arena skirmish.
- **Three vendors scouted before you start:** one selling items you can put on your list, one stocking **every** reagent your listed poison needs, and one stocking **only some** of them.
- **Bank access**, and **a loading screen you can cross**: a boat, a zeppelin, or an instance portal.
- **Something to fight**, any open-world mob or a target dummy.
- **In your bags:** a buff food and at least two plain foods of different quality, with no Well Fed buff on you.
- **Equipped:** one piece of gear under 20% durability.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional localization step at the end.

Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat with no target selected**.

## Verify this release's changes

Since the last release (2026.08.30.A), the Restocker's saved data moved to new names, with a one-time bridge that carries your Restock Lists, each character's choice of list, and the window's position across. The same bridge repairs Classic Era Rogue lists where the List Builder had saved Blinding Powder as Infantry Gauntlets. The List Builder now measures a stack by each item's real stack size. Restock List rows and adds no longer get stuck waiting on items the game had not loaded yet, and switching lists at a vendor you opened with Shift no longer buys anything. A new option adds Demonic and Dark Runes to the Mana Gem macro, and the Ignore List now reaches Rogue poisons. The Readiness Report shows its Soulstone line to Warlocks only, reads your talents a new way, and now speaks inside an arena with only what the preparation room can fix. The mini-map tooltip lists short restocking orders item by item. Every options page moved its dropdowns onto indented, captioned lines of their own and gained a tooltip on every control. And Diagnostic Tools can now check every item and spell ID the add-on ships. Run these first, on both flavors.

**Restock Lists carry across the upgrade**

**1.** On the character that was using 2026.08.30.A, log in with this build. No Lua error may appear. Type `/crs`: the window must open **where you left it, at the size you left it**, the **List** selector in its footer must show the list this character was using, and every row must keep its Amount and its Take, Store, Buy, Extra, Rep and Upgrade settings. Open the **List** selector: both of your lists must be there. Log in on the second character: it must still be on the list it followed. If you have a character that has not logged in since before 2026.08.30.A, log it in too: its lists must come back the same way. Failure is an empty list, the window back in its default spot at its default size, or either character dropped onto a fresh list.

**2.** *Rogue, level 34 or higher.* If this Rogue ticked **Blinding Powder** in the List Builder on an earlier release, its Restock List carried an **Infantry Gauntlets** row by mistake. With this build, that row must read **Blinding Powder**, with the same Amount and toggles, and no Infantry Gauntlets row may remain. Either way, click **Open List Builder** in the `/crs` window and hover **Blinding Powder**: its tooltip must name Blinding Powder, and the row it adds, or already holds, must be Blinding Powder. **This one differs by flavor:** on TBC Anniversary the List Builder must not offer Blinding Powder at all. Failure is Infantry Gauntlets anywhere on the list, a Blinding Powder row whose name stays blank, or Blinding Powder offered on TBC Anniversary.

**List Builder stack sizes**

**3.** *Paladin or Shaman, level 30 or higher.* Click **Open List Builder** in the `/crs` window. Untick **Divinity Symbol** (Paladin) or **Ankh** (Shaman) if it is already ticked, then hover the stack dropdown beside it: it must read "How many stacks to keep stocked, where one stack is N.", where N is what that item really stacks to. Set it to **1 Stack** and tick the box: the new Restock List row must want exactly N. **The Ankh differs by flavor: N is 5 on Classic Era and 10 on TBC Anniversary.** A Symbol of Divinity is 5. Right after a fresh login a tooltip may read "Loading ID: ..." for a moment, but it must fill in by itself. Failure is 20 on either item, the same Ankh number on both flavors, or a tooltip that never stops loading.

**Items the game hasn't loaded yet**

**4.** Quit the game completely, start it again, log in, and type `/crs` as soon as you can. A row may show a question-mark icon at first, but every row must fill in its icon and name within a few seconds **with the window left open**, and none may stay a question mark. Then type the ID of an item not on your list, such as 159 for Refreshing Spring Water, into the box reading "Drop item here, or type item ID" and press Enter: a named row must appear within a few seconds. Dropping an item from your bags adds it too; Shift + Click is deliberately not a way to add. Failure is a row stuck on a question mark, a typed ID that never becomes a row, or a row that only shows its name after the window is reopened.

**Switching lists at a vendor**

**5.** Give two of your lists each a different item that one vendor sells, with **Buy** on and a target above what you carry. Hold Shift while opening that vendor: nothing may be bought. With the vendor still open, open the `/crs` window if it isn't already, wait a second, and pick the other list from the **List** selector: **still nothing may be bought.** Close the vendor, open it again **without** Shift and let it buy, then switch lists again: this time the list you switch to must buy its item. Failure is anything bought on the Shift visit, or nothing bought after switching lists on a normal visit.

**Mana runes in the Mana Gem macro**

**6.** On any character other than your Mage, carrying a Demonic Rune or Dark Rune and no Mana Gem, open **Options > Connoisseur > Macros**. Under the new **Mana Gems & Runes** heading, **Add Demonic & Dark Runes to Mana Gem Macro** must be **off**, and pressing `- Mana Gem` must print "Connoisseur // No suitable Mana Gem found in your bags." and leave the rune alone. Tick it: within a second the macro must show the rune, and one press must use exactly one rune. On the Mage, tick the option there too, and with both a Mana Gem and a rune in your bags, one press must use only one item, never both. Then get rid of the gem so only the rune is left, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** under **Diagnostic Tools**, and press **Show Readiness Report**: it must still list **Mana Gem** under Missing Items, because a rune is not a gem. Failure is the rune used while the option is off, two items used by one press, or the Mage's Mana Gem line going quiet over a rune.

**The Ignore List reaches poisons**

**7.** *Rogue.* Hover the mini-map button and note the poison listed under **Main Hand** and **Off Hand** in the Attention Rogues block. Open **Options > Connoisseur > Ignore List**, pick your character on the left, type that poison's item ID into **Add by Item ID** (or Shift + Click its link in chat while the box has focus), and press Enter. Every hand that used it must move to the next rank you carry, or read **None** with "No suitable Poison found in your bags." beneath it, and `- Poisons` must stop applying it. Log out fully and back in: the poison must still be on your Ignore List. Failure is the macro still applying the ignored poison, or the entry gone after logging back in.

**The Readiness Report**

**8.** *Group with your second player on a Warlock, standing together.* On a character of yours that is **not** a Warlock, with no Soulstone up on anyone, open **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and press **Show Readiness Report**: **Soulstone Inactive** must **not** appear. Now log in your own Warlock, group with them, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** again and press the button: "Missing Buffs : Soulstone Inactive" must appear, even with a Soulstone in your bags. Use it on your second player and press again: the line must go quiet. Last, switch the report on and tick **Current Spec** on the **Readiness Report** page, then press the button once more: the Character line must name your biggest talent tree with your point spread, such as "Character : Destruction (0/21/30)", matching your talent window **on both flavors**. Failure is the Soulstone line on the non-Warlock, a line that ignores a stone in use or counts one in your bags, or a spec that is missing, wrong, or all zeros.

**9.** *TBC Anniversary only, two level-70 characters.* Queue an arena skirmish with your second player. In the preparation room, with the report switched on, tick **Damaged Gear Below**, **Current Spec**, **PvP Flag On** and **Healing Potion** on the Readiness Report page, keep your damaged gear on and no healing potion in your bags, then press **Show Readiness Report**. It must name your damaged gear and your spec, and must say **nothing** about missing buffs, expiring buffs, missing items or your PvP flag. Failure is a Missing Buffs, Missing Items or PvP line inside the arena, or no Damaged Gear line while your gear is damaged.

**The mini-map tooltip**

**10.** With between one and eight items on your Restock List short in your bags, each with **Buy** on, hover the mini-map button. Under the **Restocker Report** heading each short item must get its own row: its icon and name on the left, and what you carry against what you want on the right, such as "3/20". Make more than eight short and hover again: the rows must give way to "N Orders Outstanding" beside the heading. Fill or remove every short row and hover again: only the green "Congratulations, you're fully stocked up!" may show, with no heading above it. On the same tooltip, **Current Food** must sit on a line of its own with your food on the row beneath it, and with no food in your bags only "No suitable Food found in your bags." may appear under it. Failure is a count where rows belong, more than eight rows, a heading over the congratulation, or "None" beside Current Food.

**The options pages**

**11.** Open **Options > Connoisseur > Macros**. Turn **Prioritize Buff Food**, **Include Scroll Buffs**, **Use Pet Food Buffs** and **Re-Apply Expiring Buffs** on and off one at a time: turning each on must reveal its dropdown on an **indented line of its own** with a silver caption, **When to Use** under the first three and **Threshold** under the fourth, and turning it off must remove that line without leaving a gap. On a Druid, **Enable DruidMacroHelper Integration** reveals **Return Form** the same way. The **Explosives** dropdown must sit beside its **Click Layout** label on one line. Hover any checkbox or dropdown on the page: each must show a tooltip. Last, with only **Prioritize Buff Food** left on, set its **When to Use** to **Only When in a Party or Raid**: alone, `- Food` must offer your plain food; when your second player joins your group it must switch to your buff food within a second, and switch back when they leave. Failure is a dropdown beside or above its checkbox, a missing caption, a gap left after unticking, a control with no tooltip, or a Food macro that ignores the group change.

**12.** Open the **Restocker** page: each of the three reminder toggles must span the full width, with an indented **Detail** dropdown on the line beneath it that disappears when its toggle is off. Open the **Readiness Report** page and switch the report on: **Buffs Expiring Within** and **Damaged Gear Below** must each show an indented **Time Left** or **Durability** dropdown only while that switch is ticked, and no description line may sit under any switch, since each explains itself on hover. **Flask or 2x Elixirs** must be absent on Classic Era and present on TBC Anniversary. On the root **Connoisseur** page, every **Feedback & Support** row must keep its label and its whole URL box on one line. Failure is a dropdown showing under a switch that is off, a description line under a switch, the Flask switch on Classic Era, or a label and its box split across two lines.

**Validate Data**

**13.** Open **Diagnostic Tools** and tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**. Between **Library Versions** and **Taint Log** there must be one section per data file, from "Validate Data: Bandages.lua" to "Validate Data: Data.lua". Press **Validate Data** under "Validate Data: Food-and-Water.lua" and keep walking: the box may count up as "Validated N / M items..." and must end in a table whose every row starts with **OK** or **NOT ON CLIENT**, and the game must never stall. NOT ON CLIENT is expected for items from a later expansion than your client. Then press it under "Validate Data: Mana-Gems.lua": the **Dark Rune** and **Demonic Rune** rows must read OK. Failure is a Lua error, a stall, a box stuck on its count, or either rune marked NOT ON CLIENT.

When steps 1-13 pass on both flavors, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**14.** Log in with Connoisseur enabled. No Lua error window and no red text; a colored welcome line must print in the shape "Connoisseur // Version ...". Type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the welcome line must print again. Failure is any error naming Connoisseur, or no welcome line.

**15.** Open the options from **every** entry point: type `/foodie`, Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, type `/crs config`, and pick Connoisseur from the Blizzard Options > AddOns category list. Each must open the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Connoisseur selected in the category list on the left, showing Macros, Ignore List, Restocker, Readiness Report, Profiles and Diagnostic Tools beneath it. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a tester who runs only Era has not finished.

**16.** Pull a mob. While in combat, try `/foodie`, `/crs config` and Shift + Middle-Click: each must print "Connoisseur // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat." and the panel must **not** open. `/crs` on its own must still open the Restocker window, because only the Options route is gated. Press a Connoisseur macro mid-fight: it must use its item with no error. Now leave combat: the options panel must **not** open by itself, and any macro change that waited on the fight must land within a second or two. Failure is the panel opening, silence instead of the message, a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error, or a macro left stale after the fight.

**17.** Work through the mini-map button's clicks against its tooltip. Left-Click flips Buff Food, Shift + Left-Click flips Scroll Buffs, Right-Click ignores your current food and the **Current Food** row switches to the next best, Middle-Click clears this character's Ignore List instantly with no confirmation (deliberate), and Shift + Middle-Click opens the options. After each toggle the tooltip's Enabled or Disabled must flip while you are still hovering, and must agree with the Macros page. Then untick **Enable Mini-map Button** on the root Connoisseur page: the button must vanish at once, and ticking it must bring it straight back, with no `/reload`. Failure is any click doing something its tooltip doesn't say, the two views disagreeing, or a button that needs a reload to hide or return.

**18.** Type `/crs`: the Restocker window must toggle open and closed. `/crs show` must open it without toggling. `/crs help` must print every command with a description, no raw keys. `/crs profile` with nothing after it must print usage lines rather than erroring. Failure is a Lua error or a command that silently does nothing.

**19.** Loot, buy, or trade yourself a food better than your current pick. Within about a second the `- Food` macro, in the **General** macro tab, must rewrite to the new item, and the tooltip's Current Food row must agree. Now type `/macro` to open the game's macro window and, with it still open, eat or destroy the last of your current best food: `- Food` must **not** change while the window is open, and must rewrite to your next best food within a second of closing it. Then empty every food out of your bags and press `- Food`: chat must print "Connoisseur // No suitable Food found in your bags.", and each macro prints the same shape with its own category name when its category is empty. Failure is a macro that only updates after a `/reload`, a macro still stale after the macro window closes, macros landing in the character-specific tab, silence on an empty category, or the wrong category label.

**20.** *Warlock.* Read the `/cast` lines in the `- Healthstone` and `- Soulstone` macro bodies, then Right-Click each macro. **On Classic Era the spell must be a bare full name**, such as `Create Healthstone (Minor)`, with no `(Rank N)`. **On TBC Anniversary it must be rank-pinned**, as in `Create Healthstone(Rank 3)`. A wrong name on Classic Era makes the Right-Click **silently do nothing**, so test the click itself on both flavors rather than only reading the body. Failure is a `(Rank N)` on Classic Era, a missing rank on TBC Anniversary, or a Right-Click that creates nothing.

**21.** With **more** of a Restock List item than your target, open a vendor: **nothing may ever be sold**, so check bags and money before and after. With **less** than target and Buy on, open a vendor who stocks it: Connoisseur must buy up to the target and never past it. Hold Shift while opening the vendor: restocking must be skipped entirely. Then, on the Rogue, open the vendor stocking **every** reagent your listed poison needs: each one buys. Open the vendor stocking **only some**: nothing may be bought, and chat prints "Connoisseur // This merchant doesn't stock every ingredient your poisons need. Skipping them all." Failure is any item leaving your bags at a merchant, over-buying, Shift being ignored, or half a recipe filling your bags.

**22.** With an item set to **Take** and short in your bags, open the bank: the shortfall must move from bank to bags, ending with the "Restocking complete..." chat line. With an item set to **Store** and more in your bags than its Amount, the surplus must move to the bank. Hold Shift while opening the bank: nothing moves. Failure is a silent finish, nothing moving, or Shift being ignored.

**23.** On the **Restocker** page, set the **Detail** dropdown under **Enable At-Merchant Restock Reminders** to **Verbose**, then close a merchant window with something still short. Chat must print the "N restocking orders outstanding." headline followed by one line per short item in the shape "3/20 [item]", each a complete line with a working, clickable item link, no `nil`, no stray `%s` or `%d`, and no half-rendered link. Then cross a loading screen without visiting a merchant or the bank: no restocking line may print as the world loads. Failure is per-item lines missing in Verbose, a broken link, or the headline turning up on arrival from a boat.

**24.** Open **Diagnostic Tools** on a fresh login: the **Enable Diagnostic Tools** toggle must be **off**, and off again after a `/reload`, since it is deliberately session-only. Tick it, click **Start Event Log**, spam a red combat error by pressing an ability that isn't ready over and over, then click **Show Captured Events**: the spam must **not** appear as individual timestamped lines. Read the `-- Suppressed (uncorrelated) traffic --` block at the end, where it must fold into one counted row such as `x12`. Failure is diagnostics surviving a reload, or spam flooding the log line by line.

**25.** *Optional, non-English client.* Read the options pages, the mini-map tooltip, the Restocker window and the List Builder. Every label and tooltip must render in that language with no raw key like `OPTIONS_MODE_CAPTION` on screen. This release's new copy needs the closest look: the indented captions (When to Use, Threshold, Return Form, Detail, Time Left, Durability), the Mana Gems & Runes section, the Restocker Report rows, and the List Builder's stack tooltips. No caption may push its dropdown onto a line of its own, and no Restocker column heading may grow so wide that it crushes the item name beside it. Trigger a few chat lines, the welcome line, a "No suitable ... found" line, the poison-reagent skip line from step 21, and a Readiness Report line: each must read as one complete sentence with no `nil` and no stray `%s` or `%d`, and a Readiness Report line must separate its items with that language's own punctuation. The slash commands `/foodie` and `/crs`, the /Commands heading, the name Restocker, the four URLs, the names Discord, GitHub, CurseForge and Wago, and the whole Diagnostic Tools page stay **English on every client**. That is deliberate, not a missed translation.

This plan deliberately skips the deep per-class macro matrix (Mage conjure ranks and Ritual of Refreshment, Hunter Feed Pet's full cascade, Rogue poison hands, Druid DruidMacroHelper wraps and their return form, Night Elf stealth eating), Restock List upgrades on level-up, the entering-town reminder, per-character settings scope, the Profiles panel, target and group changes made mid-fight, and macros saved by an older release before this one rewrites them. Refresh coverage there when a release touches those systems.

When every step passes on **both** Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
